Claim the playfield one line at a time. Leave the safety of the border and draw a line through open space; close it back onto the border and the side without the enemy is yours. Claim enough of the field to clear the level.

The open field is not empty. A tangle of lines roams it, and touching it or the line you are drawing costs a life. Sparks run along the border and over your finished lines, and stopping halfway lights a fuse that burns along your line toward you. Draw fast to stay out of danger, or draw slowly for double points.

• The rules of the 1981 arcade game, with its fill animation, flickering lines and sprites
• A floating stick under one thumb and a Slow button under the other, so your fingers never cover the board
• Or follow the finger: touch the playfield and the marker heads for the point just above it
• A left-handed layout, and a choice of stick size and control opacity
• Clear a level at anything from 50 to 90 % of the board, and choose the starting lives
• Your ten best scores, with three initials each
• Haptics on turns, claims and deaths; synthesised arcade sounds if you want them, muted by the silent switch
• A demo game on the title screen and a How to play page in the settings
• Hardware keyboards and game controllers
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no account, no tracking and no analytics: the game works offline and never sends anything anywhere
